Privacy Policy

Wiser: Daily Wisdom · Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy covers Wiser: Daily Wisdom, the daily quote app published by Shrikant Kadu. It explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it stays on your phone.

What the app stores

  • Your first name, if you give one, so the app can address you by name.
  • The sages you pick when you set up the app.
  • The thoughts you save, and which thought was chosen for each day.

All of this is stored only on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask you to sign in.
  • It does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not share or sell any information, because it never receives any.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules a daily notification on your device. These are created and shown by your phone, not sent from a server. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

Sharing

When you share a quote as an image or text, the app hands it to the share sheet on your phone. What happens next depends on the app you choose, such as WhatsApp or Instagram, and is covered by that app's own policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it completely.
